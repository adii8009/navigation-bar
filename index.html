About Us

S B Patil College of Engineering is a reputed technical institute located at Indapur, Pune district, Maharashtra, established in 2009 under the aegis of Shahajirao Patil Vikas Pratishthan. The college is committed to delivering quality technical education and developing skilled engineers capable of meeting global challenges. The institute is affiliated to Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU) and is approved by All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi. It follows a curriculum that balances strong theoretical foundations with practical, industry-oriented learning. S B Patil College of Engineering offers undergraduate engineering programs in disciplines such as Computer Engineering, Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, and Electrical Engineering. The college emphasizes innovation, research, and hands-on experience through well-equipped laboratories, workshops, and project-based learning. Spread across a spacious and eco-friendly campus, the institute provides modern infrastructure, a well-stocked library, digital learning resources, and facilities that support academic, technical, and extracurricular activities. The college also focuses on overall personality development, encouraging students to participate in technical events, cultural programs, sports, and professional skill development activities. With a dedicated team of qualified faculty members and a student-centric approach, S B Patil College of Engineering strives to create responsible engineers, ethical professionals, and lifelong learners who contribute positively to society and the nation.
